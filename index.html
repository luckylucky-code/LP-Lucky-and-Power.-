About LP

Growing with purpose, one opportunity at a time.

LP (Lucky and Power) is a tumbler brand focused on quality, convenience, stylish design, and sustainability. We aim to create practical reusable products while building a workplace where professionals can learn, contribute, and grow.

Our Mission

LP is committed to providing high-quality, durable, stylish, and affordable tumblers that offer convenience, functionality, and value. We encourage responsible consumption through reusable alternatives to single-use containers and strive for innovation, excellent service, and responsible business practices.

Our Vision

LP envisions becoming a trusted and recognized tumbler brand that promotes quality, innovation, convenience, and sustainability. We aim to meet changing customer needs through continuous improvement, creative designs, and a commitment to excellence while creating a positive impact in the communities we serve.
